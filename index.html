M.I.T. · EARLY 1960s

      
"HACKER"

      
was a compliment — an elegant trick, pulled off with style.

      
was a compliment — until it became a crime. Sixty years: the word changed meaning, and a profession grew out of the wreckage.

      

        
1962Tech Model Railroad Club coins it

        
1970sphone phreaks · a toy whistle at 2600 Hz

        
1983WarGames makes it a villain

        
1988the Morris Worm makes it a crime

      

    

  


  

    
    

      
TRUE STORY · NOVEMBER 2, 1988

      
0MACHINES INFECTED

      
The Morris Worm was meant to be harmless. A coding mistake made it reinfect machines until they choked — roughly 10% of the young internet, down in a night.

      
Out of that wreckage a job appeared: break in first — politely, with permission, and with a written report.

    

  


  

    

      
THE DEFINITION, STRIPPED OF FOLKLORE

      

        HACKINGISGAININGUNAUTHORIZEDACCESSTOASYSTEM,NETWORKORDEVICE.
      

      
It doesn't say clever. It doesn't say evil. It runs from harmless curiosity to organized crime — the access is the constant.

    

  


  

    

      
EVERY HACKER IS ONE POINT ON TWO QUESTIONS

      

        
        
WERE THEY ALLOWED? · WHAT DID THEY WANT?

      

      
The hat colors come from cowboy films — the hero wore white, the villain black.

    

  


  

    

      
THE DEFINITION THAT LOOKS LIKE A CONTRADICTION

      

        AUTHORIZED
        attempt to gain
        UNAUTHORIZED
        access
      

      
Read it twice and it stops being a paradox: the access would be unauthorized — except the owner signed permission first. Also called penetration testing or red teaming.

      
Same tools as the criminal. Opposite mission: get in, get out, write everything down.

    

  


  

    

      
THE THREE THINGS EVERY ATTACK TRIES TO BREAK

      

        
C
CONFIDENTIALITY
secrets stay secret

        
I
INTEGRITY
data stays correct

        
A
AVAILABILITY
systems stay up

      

      
For any attack in any exam answer, ask which letter it breaks. Stealing data = C. Changing data = I. Deleting or blocking = A. Ransomware hits all three in one move.

    

  


  

    

      
SIX TERMS, ONE CHAIN REACTION

      

        
VULNERABILITYthe unlocked window — a weakness

        
THREATthe burglar on the street

        
EXPLOITthe brick through the window

        
RISKlikelihood × damage

        
ATTACK SURFACEevery door, window & employee

      

      
The CIA triad completes the family: the three things worth breaking.

    

  


  

    

      
WHAT MAKES IT ETHICAL — SEVEN PRINCIPLES, ONE SENTENCE

      

        GET PERMISSIONKEEP SECRETSTELL THE TRUTHBREAK NOTHINGOWN YOUR ACTIONSHIDE NOTHINGKEEP LEARNING
      

      
ILLEGAL WITHOUT IT — IT ACT, 2000

      
Skill doesn't separate the white hat from the black hat — the tools are identical. The code of conduct does. In India, unauthorized access is punishable under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

THE SEVEN
PHASES

    
real spies gather maps carefully, record repairs

  


  

    
    

      
PHASE 1 / 7 · RECONNAISSANCE

      
Know the target before you touch it.

      

        
PASSIVEWHOIS · Shodan · Google dorkinginvisible — you never touch the target

        
ACTIVEpings · probes · port scanslouder — your packets land in their logs

      

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 2 / 7 · SCANNING

      
Recon asks what's out there.
Scanning asks where it hurts.

      

      
Open ports · running services · known vulnerabilities — Nmap, Nessus, OpenVAS, Nikto.

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 3 / 7 · GAINING ACCESS

      
The break-in the films show.

      

        

        
user→root

      

      
A found vulnerability is exploited to enter — then privilege escalation climbs from an ordinary account to root or admin. Workhorse tool: Metasploit.

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 4 / 7 · MAINTAINING ACCESS

      
Getting in was hard.
Never do it twice.

      

      
Backdoors, rootkits, hidden accounts — ways back in that survive password changes and reboots. The white hat plants them documented, then removes every one.

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 5 / 7 · CLEARING TRACKS — THE UNETHICAL PHASE

      
Delete every trace.

      
[02:13:04] sshd: accepted password for admin from 10.0.0.66
[02:13:41] sudo: admin → root · command: /bin/bash
[02:15:22] auth: session opened for user root
[02:17:09] xfer: 412 MB → 45.155.██.██
[02:18:31] sshd: session closed

      
Your slides say it plainly: this phase is actually an unethical activity. It's taught so you know what an attacker erases — and how to protect logs with central, off-box logging.

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 6 / 7 · REPORTING

      
The phase that pays.

      

        
PENETRATION TEST — FINDINGS

        

      

      
Document what was found, exactly how it was exploited, what was reached, the business impact, and the fix. A test without a report is just a crime with extra steps — this document is the whole difference.

    

  


  

    

      
PHASE 7 / 7 · REMEDIATION + RETEST

      
Close the loop.

      

        
        
patch → reconfigure → attack it again

      

      
A fix without a retest is a hope, not a control. Then the whole lifecycle loops — systems, threats and tools all move on. Security is a cycle, not a project.

    

  


  
  

    
03

    
THE
CONTRACT

    
penetration testing — the break-in, bought in advance

  


  

    

      
PENTEST, WORD BY WORD

      

        
FOCUSEDnamed systems, named dates, named limits

        
CONTROLLEDrules of engagement; it never becomes the disaster it simulates

        
AUTHORIZEDwritten permission, signed first — the line between consultant and convict

      

      
…a simulation of a real cyberattack, to identify and exploit vulnerabilities — with proof, not plunder, as the goal.

    

  


  

    

      
THE COUNTDOWN EVERY DEFENDER FEARS

      
?
DAYS TO WRITE THE FIX

      
A zero-day is exploited before the vendor knows it exists — zero days to patch. Stuxnet burned four in one weapon. Equifax lost 147 million records to a flaw whose patch had sat unapplied for two months.

    

  


  

    

      
HOW MUCH IS THE TESTER TOLD?

      

        

        

          
BLACK BOXknows nothing — the stranger off the street

          
GRAY BOXa normal user account — the insider, or one phished password

          
WHITE BOXsource code + configs — the auditor with the blueprints

        

      

      
Testing a bank vault: black = walk in off the street. Gray = open a savings account first. White = the manager hands you the blueprints.

    

  


  

    

      
THE LIFECYCLE, WEARING A SUIT — 5 PHASES UNDER CONTRACT

      

        
1 · PLAN + RECONscope, goals, intelligence

        
2 · SCANNikto, Nessus, Nmap sweep for known flaws

        
3 · EXPLOITpassword cracking, SQL injection, social engineering

        
4 · MAINTAINshow how far the damage could reach

        
5 · REPORTwhat, how, how bad, and the fix

      

      
The exam favorite: a vulnerability assessment lists holes. A penetration test proves them.

    

  


  
  

    
04

    
SEEING THE
NETWORK

    
nmap — one article in 1997, still the first tool opened

  


  

    

      
TRUE STORY · 2003

      

        trinity@grid:~$ ▊
      

      
In The Matrix Reloaded, Trinity disables a power grid with a real tool: nmap — finds SSH on port 22, and exploits it.

      
Born in Phrack magazine, September 1997: "The Art of Port Scanning", by Fyodor. Security people cheered in the theater; Hollywood finally used the actual command.

    

  


  

    

      
TWO ADDRESS SYSTEMS, STACKED

      
The IP finds the building.
The port finds the door.

      

        
      

      
Own these: 21 FTP · 22 SSH · 23 Telnet · 25 SMTP · 53 DNS · 80 HTTP · 443 HTTPS · 445 SMB · 3306 MySQL · 3389 RDP

    

  


  

    

      
THE HANDSHAKE EVERY SCANNER EXPLOITS

      
      

        
        
      

      
A connect scan finishes the handshake with every port — and every finished connection can be logged. The SYN scan hears SYN-ACK ("open"), then walks away with an RST: half-open, and much quieter.

    

  


  

    

      
EVERY PORT ANSWERS ONE OF THREE WAYS

      

        
OPENa service is listening — every one is a possible way in

        
CLOSED"nobody lives here" — but the machine is alive

        
FILTEREDa firewall pretends you don't exist — nmap can't tell

      

      
The doorman model: the door opens · "nobody here", said from inside · a guard who ignores you. Attackers love the first, note the second, curse the third.

    

  


  

    

      
THE COMMAND LINE, ASSEMBLED IN THE AIR

      
$ nmap ▊

      

        
-sSSYN stealth — the professional default

        
-sVversion detection — "Apache 2.4.49", not just "80 open"

        
-OOS fingerprint from TTL / window / DF / TOS

        
-Aaggressive: OS + versions + scripts + traceroute. loud.

        
-Ffast — 100 most common ports

        
-ppick ports: exact · list · range · service names

        
/24the whole subnet — 256 addresses

      

      
Anatomy: nmap names the machine, -p picks the doors, -F makes it quick, -A makes it thorough, -O names the OS, -sV names the software. Default scan = 1000 ports.

    

  


  

    

      
READ A REPORT LIKE AN EXAMINER

      
$ nmap -A scanme.nmap.org
Host is up (0.21s latency).
Not shown: 996 closed tcp ports (reset)
PORT     STATE    SERVICE     VERSION
22/tcp   open     ssh         OpenSSH 6.6.1p1
80/tcp   open     http        Apache httpd 2.4.7
9929/tcp open     nping-echo  Nping echo
OS details: Linux 3.2 - 4.9
1 host up · scanned in 21.4 seconds

      
996 boring doors hidden, three interesting ones printed. Port + state + service + exact version — because vulnerabilities attach to versions. scanme.nmap.org is run by the nmap project itself for legal practice.

    

  


  

    

      
THE LAW OF THE SCAN

      
NO PERMISSION = CRIME

      
Scanning without authorization is illegal and unethical, full stop — punishable under the IT Act, 2000. The same action is a professional service with a signature, and an offence without one.

      
Legal practice targets, built for exactly this: scanme.nmap.org · testphp.vulweb.com
